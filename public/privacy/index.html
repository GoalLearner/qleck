Qleck Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Qleck is an AI goal coach: you tell it a goal, it gives you an honest read on your odds, and it builds a step-by-step plan you check in against. This page explains exactly what Qleck stores and why. The short version: we store what you give us so the app works, we don't sell it, we don't run ads, we don't track you, and you can erase all of it from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Your account. On iOS you sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a random identifier for your Apple ID — that identifier is your account. We do not collect or store your Apple ID email address or the name on your Apple account. On the web version there is no sign-in; an anonymous device identity is created instead.
  • Your profile. The display name and handle you type in, if you choose to set them.
  • Your goals and check-ins. The goal text you write, the answers you give the coach, the plan it builds, and the check-ins and measurements you log against it. This is the product — without it there is nothing to coach.
  • Your timezone, so streaks roll over at your midnight, not ours.

How the AI coaching works

When you describe a goal or answer the coach's questions, that text is sent to our server and processed by a third-party AI model provider to generate the coach's questions, verdicts and plans. The text you write is used only to produce your coaching — not for advertising and not to build a profile of you. Don't include information in your goals you wouldn't want processed this way.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or renting your data to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No reading your contacts, photos, location, or health data.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited to what's needed to run the service.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the You tab and choose Delete account. This permanently erases your account and everything attached to it — profile, goals, plans, check-ins, and your conversations with the coach — from our servers, immediately. There is no undo. You can also email us at the address below and we'll do it for you.

Children

Qleck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
